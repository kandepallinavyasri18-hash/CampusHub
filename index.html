How it works

Get started in three steps. Authentication and database storage are already in place.

  1. 1

    Create your account

    Register with your full name, email and a password, and pick your role: Student or Faculty.

  2. 2

    Log in securely

    Your password is hashed before it is stored, and you are redirected to the dashboard for your role.

  3. 3

    Start sharing

    Use your dashboard to reach resources, questions, announcements and your profile.

Roles in CampusHub

Each role sees a different dashboard, and access is enforced on the server.

🎓 Student

Search resources, manage your own uploads, ask questions and follow announcements.

📚 Faculty

Upload and manage teaching resources, answer questions and publish announcements.

🔒 Admin

Manage users, resources, categories, announcements and platform statistics.

Admin accounts are created separately by an administrator — the option is not available on the public registration form.
